01 · Context

Problem Overview

Pregnancy · Postpartum · Menopause

Pregnancy, postpartum recovery, and menopause are not just life stages — they are powerful biological transitions. Nutritional needs change rapidly, energy fluctuates, digestion shifts, and emotional well-being becomes closely tied to food. Yet most diet platforms still ship rigid, one-size-fits-all plans — ignoring body constitution, nutrient absorption, symptom severity, and the simple truth that taste preferences change over time.

1

Why it matters

  • Women in pregnancy, postpartum, and menopause face nutritional and digestive needs that shift with hormonal change.
  • Poor nutrition and incompatible food combinations raise the risk of anemia, bone loss, and metabolic disorders.
  • Early intervention prevents long-term complications.
2

Existing solutions

  • Calorie-based diet and fitness apps
  • Basic pregnancy tracking apps
  • Standalone Ayurveda guidance without data-driven compatibility analysis
  • Manual nutrition charts handed out by doctors or dieticians
3

Gap in the ecosystem

  • No unified platform integrates life-phase nutrition, Ayurveda, and nutrigenomics.
  • Diet systems fail to adapt to individual absorption patterns and hormonal change.
  • No intelligent meal-compatibility analysis or explainable scoring.

Women deserve nutrition that evolves with them.

02 · Solution

Proposed Solution

Compatibility-aware, life-phase adaptive nutrition

Prakriva is a compatibility-aware nutrition platform that generates personalized, life-phase adaptive meal plans for women using real-time food data, Ayurvedic principles, and structured compatibility logic. It combines multi-food analysis, API-driven recipe intelligence, and interactive 3D visualization to deliver explainable nutrition planning.

Key capabilities

  • Multi-food compatibility scoring engine
  • 3D meal compatibility visualization on a human model
  • Real-time RecipeDB + FlavorDB integration (790K+ recipes)
  • Ayurveda and body-constitution personalization
  • Life-phase adaptive nutrition — pregnancy, postpartum, menopause
  • Structured, explainable meal-plan generation

Target users

  • Women in pregnancy, postpartum, and menopause
  • Individuals seeking compatibility-aware nutrition
  • Ayurvedic practitioners and nutritionists
  • Health-conscious users wanting structured meal planning

Value proposition

Prakriva goes beyond calorie tracking to deliver compatibility-driven, constitution-based, and life-phase adaptive nutrition — powered by live global food intelligence.

03 · Approach

From tradition to precision

Ayurveda · AI · Genomics as one loop

1

Ayur-Nutrigenomics Insights

We begin by analyzing the patient's dosha (Ayurveda) and genetic markers (nutrigenomics) to understand how their body responds to nutrients — a deeply personalized foundation for diet planning.

2

LLM-Powered Meal Drafts

An LLM generates intelligent, ready-to-use meal-plan drafts from the patient profile. Doctors review and refine them with a drag-and-drop interface.

3

Doctor Co-Learning Memory

Every doctor modification is recorded, so the AI learns from expert decisions over time — continuously improving alignment with real-world clinical expertise.

4

Patient Feedback Loop

Patients report effectiveness and satisfaction after following a plan. That feedback drives reinforcement learning, closing the loop between patient, doctor, and AI.

Patient profile→ Dosha + genetic markers→ LLM draft plan→ Doctor refinement→ Patient feedback↺ Smarter next plan

07 · Workflow

Request → plan, in six steps

790K recipes filtered down to one 7-day plan

Client & services

  • Web application requests a diet plan
  • API Gateway orchestrates the pipeline
  • Dosha Predictor classifies constitution
  • Food Filter narrows the candidate pool
  • Food Scorer ranks compatibility
  • Meal Plan Generator assembles the week
  • Final plan saved to Firebase Firestore

Funnel

790K recipesRecipeDB corpus
↓ apply filters — diet, calories, protein, cuisine
200–300 candidatesFiltering process
↓ score & check compatibility
Top 80 recipesRanked by weighted score
↓ GPT-4 meal planning
7-day planReturned to the user with explanations

Scoring weights

  • Dosha compatibility — 30%
  • Nutrition fit — 25%
  • Flavor pairing — 20%
  • Health conditions — 15%
  • User preference — 10%

Compatibility check

FlavorDB validates ingredient pairing, Ayurvedic tastes, and food combinations — flagging Viruddha Ahara before a meal reaches the plan.
